# Business information and online presence

## Business information

This section supplies the main content of your public listing. Complete the business name, short description, **About** text, and service description in both English and French.

Before saving, confirm that:

- the business name matches your public brand;
- the description explains what makes your business useful to couples;
- dates, locations, and service details are current; and
- both language versions communicate the same facts.

## Address and Google reviews

In **Contact & Online Presence**, add the business address when applicable. If you do not have a customer-facing address, provide at least the country, province, and city.

Location information helps the platform find the associated Google Business Profile and display its review score. Contact the site administrator if you do not want reviews and stars displayed.

## Online links

Add the correct URLs for your Instagram, website, and Facebook pages. These links appear in the contact card on the public profile, so test each one after saving.
