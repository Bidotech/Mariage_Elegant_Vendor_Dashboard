# Profile and subscription

## Profile

Use **Profile** to add the name and phone number used for communication with the site administrator. These administrative contact details are not shown to other users.

## Choose a subscription

The **Subscription** section keeps your vendor listing visible on the platform. Available plans shown in the dashboard include:

- **RADIANCE** — six months - 695CAD; and
- **ETERNAL SHINE** — one year - 895CAD.

You may purchase a plan online or contact the administrator to arrange payment manually.

## Invoices and plan history

After payment, allow a few hours for the invoice to appear. The subscription history lists the purchase date, expiry date, price, user, and available invoice PDF.

Use **Upgrade plan** to move to another plan. Review the consequences before selecting **Cancel subscription**.

## Pricing

To have your listing active on Elegant Wedding you need to subscribe to the RADIANCE or ETERNAL SHINE. RADIANCE keeps your listing active for 6 month and ETERNAL SHINE keeps it for one year. RADIANCE is 695CAD and ETERNAL SHINE is 895CAD. You may purchase a plan online or contact the administrator to arrange payment manually.
