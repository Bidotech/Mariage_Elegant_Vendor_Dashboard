# Services and gallery

## Services

The **Services** page is separate from the service description in **Business Information**. Use it to select the structured services and answer the additional questions that apply to your business.

Choose only services you currently provide. Update the selections whenever your offering changes.

## Gallery requirements

Use **Gallery** to upload images that represent your business accurately.

- Each image must be smaller than **2 MB**.
- You can upload up to **30 images**.
- The first four images appear at the top of the public profile.
- For the best first-row display, make the first four images square at **512 × 512 pixels**.

## Reorder and review

Drag and drop uploaded images to change their order. The remaining images are available through **Full Gallery**.

<figure className="screenshot">
  <img src={require('@site/static/img/vendor-dashboard/011.jpg').default} alt="Full Gallery link beside the main vendor gallery preview" />
  <figcaption>Visitors can open Full Gallery to see the rest of the uploaded images.</figcaption>
</figure>

Changes may take a short time to appear. When finished, open **View Your Live Profile** and verify the crop, order, and image quality on both desktop and mobile.
