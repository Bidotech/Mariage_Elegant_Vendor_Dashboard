# Connect Instagram

Connect an **Instagram Business** account to display recent posts on the vendor profile. The platform retrieves the 10 most recent posts and displays four of them.

Posts containing **#MariageElegant** receive priority. If fewer than four recent posts use the hashtag, the newest remaining posts complete the set. New posts may take up to seven days to appear.

## Connect the account

1. Open **Instagram** in the dashboard.
2. Select **Connect to Instagram**.

<figure className="screenshot">
  <img src={require('@site/static/img/vendor-dashboard/012.jpg').default} alt="Instagram dashboard page with Connect to Instagram button" />
  <figcaption>Start the connection from the Instagram page in the vendor dashboard.</figcaption>
</figure>

3. In the official Instagram or Meta authorization window, sign in to the Instagram Business account.

<figure className="screenshot">
  <img src={require('@site/static/img/vendor-dashboard/013.jpg').default} alt="Official Instagram login form" />
  <figcaption>Enter credentials only in the official Instagram or Meta authorization window.</figcaption>
</figure>

4. Review the account and application details, then select **Allow** to authorize the connection.

<figure className="screenshot">
  <img src={require('@site/static/img/vendor-dashboard/014.jpg').default} alt="Instagram authorization screen with Allow and Cancel options" />
  <figcaption>Authorization permits the application to retrieve posts for the vendor profile.</figcaption>
</figure>

:::danger[Protect your Instagram credentials]
Never send your password to the site administrator and never enter it into an unfamiliar form. If the authorization page does not use an official Instagram or Meta domain, cancel the process and contact the administrator.
:::

If the business is new to the platform, wait for administrator approval before expecting Instagram posts to appear publicly.
