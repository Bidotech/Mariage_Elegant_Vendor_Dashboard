# Leads and messages

Open **Leads** to view quote requests, customer questions, and messages from the site administrator. Use the search field to find a conversation, and select a row to open it.

<figure className="screenshot">
  <img src={require('@site/static/img/vendor-dashboard/001.jpg').default} alt="Leads menu and message list in the vendor dashboard" />
  <figcaption>The Leads page lists customer and administrator conversations.</figcaption>
</figure>

## Leads without an account

When a customer submits an inquiry without creating an account, the conversation shows their contact details. Contact the customer using the provided email address or phone number; you cannot reply to this type of lead inside the platform.

<figure className="screenshot">
  <img src={require('@site/static/img/vendor-dashboard/002-redacted.png').default} alt="Unregistered lead conversation with email and phone values redacted" />
  <figcaption>An unregistered lead includes direct contact details. Values are redacted in this public guide.</figcaption>
</figure>

:::warning[Handle contact details carefully]
Use lead contact information only to respond to the inquiry. Do not publish, forward, or reuse it for unrelated purposes.
:::

## Leads with an account

If the customer has an account, type your response in the message field and select the send button. Keep the reply clear and include the next step, such as a call, quotation, or appointment.

  <figcaption>Registered customers can receive replies directly through the Leads section.</figcaption>
