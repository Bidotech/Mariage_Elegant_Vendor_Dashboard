# Vendor Dashboard Guide

<div className="lead">
  Use this guide to keep your Mariage Élégant vendor profile accurate, visible,
  and ready for prospective clients.
</div>

The dashboard brings your messages, subscription, business details, services, gallery, and Instagram connection into one place. Use the language selector in the top navigation to switch between English and French.

:::info[Before you begin]
New vendors may need administrator approval before profile changes appear on the live website. Saving a change in the dashboard does not necessarily mean it is already public.
:::

## Recommended setup order

1. Complete your contact details in **Profile**.
2. Add your English and French business information.
3. Select the services you provide.
4. Add your address and online links.
5. Upload and reorder your gallery images.
6. Choose or renew a subscription.
7. Connect an Instagram Business account if desired.
8. Select **View Your Live Profile** and review the public result after approval.
