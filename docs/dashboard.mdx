# Dashboard and live profile

## Dashboard

The dashboard shows the latest activity related to:

- new leads;
- your subscription status; and
- messages from the site administrator.

Check it regularly so you can respond to new inquiries and notices promptly.

## View Your Live Profile

Select **View Your Live Profile** to open the current public version of your vendor listing. Use this preview after updating your business information, services, gallery, or online links.

:::note[New vendor approval]
If your business is still being verified, recent changes may not appear in the live preview until an administrator approves the profile.
:::
